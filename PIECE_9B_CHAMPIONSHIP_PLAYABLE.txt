FOOTBALL CEO — PIECE 9B: CHAMPIONSHIP PLAYABLE
Version: v0.24.26-piece-9b-championship-playable

SCOPE
- Makes all 24 2025/26 Championship clubs selectable and fully playable.
- Retains the 20 playable Premier League clubs: 44 playable clubs total.
- Championship careers use the existing full CEO simulation systems rather than the background-only club path.
- Promotion/relegation movement and playoff match execution remain disabled until their dedicated pieces.

CHAMPIONSHIP CAREER SHAPE
- 24 clubs.
- 46 league matches per club (23 home, 23 away).
- 46 rounds, 12 matches per round, 552 league matches total.
- Generated 2025/26 league calendar runs from 9 August 2025 to 2 May 2026.
- Calendar is a Football CEO schedule model rather than an attempt to reproduce the exact real-life fixture list.
- Premier League moves to background simulation when the user manages in the Championship; the user's active division is never double-simulated.

PLAYABLE METADATA
- Added 2025/26 starting manager metadata for all 24 Championship clubs, aligned to the game's 1 August 2025 start date.
- Added stadium names and capacities for all 24 Championship clubs.
- Existing embedded Championship player database is reused: 650 players total at this build's data snapshot.
- Existing financial profiles, ownership profiles, academy profiles and sporting-expectation logic are reused.

ENGINE CHANGES
- Career fixtures, tables, squad/manager pools, AI review pools and domestic transfer logic now resolve from the user's active league.
- Championship clubs in a Championship save use full domestic AI finance/recruitment rather than background-world transfer logic.
- World/background competition simulation skips the user's active league and can simulate the Premier League in a Championship career.
- New-save and imported-save club validation recognises all 44 playable clubs.
- Table styling supports Championship automatic-promotion and playoff bands.
- Dynamic sporting expectations use detailed embedded player quality for EFL clubs when available.

NOT IN PIECE 9B
- No promotion/relegation movement yet.
- No Championship playoff matches yet.
- League One and League Two remain background/non-playable.
- No National League relegation will be added; League Two remains the static bottom playable division.
