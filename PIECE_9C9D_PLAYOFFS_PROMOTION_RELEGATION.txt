FOOTBALL CEO v0.24.32 — PIECES 9C/9D
CHAMPIONSHIP PLAY-OFFS + PL/CHAMPIONSHIP PROMOTION/RELEGATION

Scope
-----
This build extends the merged v0.24.31 UI-hotfix source of truth.
It enables the first real divisional movement boundary in Football CEO:
Premier League <-> Championship.

Championship play-offs
----------------------
- 3rd to 6th qualify.
- Semi-final A: 6th vs 3rd, then 3rd vs 6th.
- Semi-final B: 5th vs 4th, then 4th vs 5th.
- Semi-finals are two-legged and decided on aggregate; tied aggregates use extra time / penalties.
- Winners meet in a neutral-venue Play-Off Final at Wembley Stadium.
- For the 2025/26 calendar the generated dates are 8/9 May, 11/12 May and 23 May 2026.
- User playoff matches use the normal detailed match engine, player usage/condition and stored lineup reports.
- Dashboard and Fixtures UI show upcoming/played playoff fixtures after MW46.
- Background Championship play-offs also execute while the user manages in the Premier League.

Wembley revenue
---------------
Reaching Wembley provides a meaningful one-off revenue boost.
The gate model does not assume a finalist keeps the whole Wembley gate. It models event costs,
the EFL levy/pool structure and the finalist club's residual gate share, then adds a separate
commercial/merchandising uplift linked to Commercial Reach.

A typical Championship finalist in the automated test receives about £1.2m incremental club
income from the final. Semi-finalists also receive their share of the pooled semi-final gate.
Playoff income is recorded in the Finance system as matchday/commercial operating revenue and
in the dedicated playoff revenue tracker.

Promotion/relegation
--------------------
- Premier League bottom 3 -> Championship.
- Championship top 2 -> Premier League automatically.
- Championship Play-Off Final winner -> Premier League.
- Movement occurs simultaneously at season rollover, preserving 20 PL and 24 Championship clubs.
- The complete club identity/state remains intact: players, contracts, finances, facilities,
  stadium, ownership, Commercial Reach, debts and transfer commitments stay with the club.
- User leagueId and player leagueIds are migrated with the club.
- Existing dynamic league-movement hooks fire for the user: sporting expectation context,
  staff promotion/relegation wage clauses and Commercial Reach/exposure refresh.
- Saved club-division overrides persist across save/load, while starting a new career restores
  the original 2025/26 divisions before club selection.
- Next-season fixtures switch correctly: promoted user = 38 PL matches; relegated user = 46 Championship matches.

Season flow
-----------
Regular league ends -> Championship play-offs -> final outcome -> 1 June season review/rollover
-> promotion/relegation movement -> new division table/fixtures -> new season budgets/expectations.

Out of scope
------------
- Championship -> League One movement remains disabled until League One is playable.
- League One and League Two play-offs remain disabled.
- League Two remains the static bottom of the playable pyramid; there is no National League relegation.
