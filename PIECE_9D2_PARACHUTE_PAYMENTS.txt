FOOTBALL CEO v0.24.34 — PIECE 9D.2 PARACHUTE PAYMENTS

Scope
- Premier League -> EFL parachute entitlement attached to the club, not the CEO.
- 55% / 45% / 20% of the current Premier League equal central distribution.
- Established PL clubs: up to three seasons. Clubs relegated after a one-season PL spell: two seasons.
- Payments stop immediately on promotion back to the Premier League.
- A later relegation creates a new entitlement; old cancelled entitlement never resumes.
- Payments are staged across weekly operating cycles and true-up safely at season end.
- Entitlement/payment state is idempotent and save-safe. Existing v0.24.33 movement history is migrated.

Current in-game values (using £88m PL equal-share base)
- Year 1: £48.4m
- Year 2: £39.6m
- Year 3: £17.6m

Finance integration
- Parachute income is its own operating-income line, not hidden inside generic league revenue.
- It counts in operating P/L and SCR revenue.
- Finance screen shows current entitlement, future schedule and promotion-cancellation warning.
- AI club financial capacity includes current-season parachute support.
- Payments enter club cash/revenue normally; they are not auto-assigned to the transfer budget.

Relegation-transition calibration
- Starting club revenue profiles are snapshots of the 2025/26 starting division.
- A PL club relegated to the Championship now loses most PL exposure before parachute support is added.
- Example test: West Ham £290m starting PL profile -> ~£90.5m Championship underlying target + £48.4m Year-1 parachute = ~£138.9m.
- A promoted Championship club receives a sensible PL revenue floor without inheriting an established giant's profile.

Out of scope
- EFL transitional/parachute payments for Championship -> League One / League One -> League Two.
- Player wage promotion/relegation clauses.
- League One/League Two playability and movement.
