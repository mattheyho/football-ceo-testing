Football CEO v0.24.22 — Development Review Fix

Restores the scheduled player-development reports on:
- 1 October (Autumn)
- 1 January (Mid-season)
- 1 April (Spring)

Fixes:
1. A development report is now created at every scheduled checkpoint even when no player crosses an OVR threshold.
2. The review pool includes the current first-team squad plus players owned by the user club who are out on loan.
3. User-owned outgoing loanees use the user/player development path (including loan minutes and loan environment) rather than the lightweight AI background path.
4. User-owned outgoing loanees are excluded from the subsequent AI-world checkpoint buckets, preventing double processing.
5. Development review rows show unchanged players and identify players who are out on loan.

No change has been made to the year-end ageing model: biological age decline remains a once-per-season calculation.
