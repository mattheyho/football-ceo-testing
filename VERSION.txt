Football CEO v0.15.0 — Daily Calendar & Simulation Engine

Core:
- Continue advances one calendar day.
- state.week remains league matchweeks completed; calendar time is separate.
- Fixtures carry actual dates generated independently every season.
- Opening round is the first Saturday on/after 10 August.
- Mix of weekend rounds, midweek rounds and breaks runs the league into May.
- Playable off-season after the final league match.
- New football season rolls over in early June.
- Monthly reports follow actual calendar months.

Simulation cadence:
- Daily: injuries/recovery, transfer-market activity, scheduled events.
- Monday: manager squad review and AI squad planning.
- Event-triggered: major sale or 14+ day injury causes manager reassessment next day.
- Sunday: wages, recurring operating costs, commercial and sponsorship accounting.
- Match date: fixture simulation, matchday revenue, appearances/goals/ratings.

Transfers:
- Summer window: 16 June–1 September.
- Winter window: 1 January–2 February.
- Old weekly transfer-window bands removed.
- Incoming/AI transfer probabilities converted from weekly to daily equivalents.
- Manager 12-week cooldowns converted to 84 calendar days.

Off-season:
- Final match opens Season Review.
- Continue from review enters off-season instead of jumping to MW1.
- Calendar continues through late May/June.
- Season rollover occurs after the May monthly report, in early June.
- Next season fixtures are regenerated from the new year's weekday calendar.

Compatibility:
- Existing week-based saves receive an inferred calendar date.
- Old week-based injuries are migrated to daysRemaining.
- Legacy advanceMatchweek() remains as an alias to advanceDay().

Patch v0.15.0a
- New careers begin on 1 August 2025.
