Football CEO v0.16.2 — External Transfer Buyers

Built on v0.16.1 Transfer Flow.

SCOPE
- Expands the transfer BUYER market without adding full foreign/EFL leagues or a large new player database.
- External clubs are lightweight market actors only.
- They have no generated squads, fixtures, league tables or domestic simulation.
- v0.16.2 does NOT add a searchable external-player database.

EXTERNAL MARKET
65 lightweight external buyer profiles:
- 24 Championship clubs.
- 9 lower-EFL clubs from League One / League Two.
- 32 foreign/other major-market clubs across Spain, Germany, France, Italy, Portugal, Netherlands, Turkey and Scotland.

Each external club has:
- reputation
- abstract seasonal transfer budget
- wage ceiling
- expected squad standard
- division / country
- player-fit logic.

BID BEHAVIOUR
- Incoming offers can now come from Premier League clubs OR external clubs.
- External buyers account for roughly half of the viable incoming market, with higher weighting for transfer-listed/fringe players.
- Transfer-listed players are especially attractive to Championship/EFL/value-seeking clubs.
- Lower-EFL clubs primarily target affordable fringe players and prospects.
- Championship clubs target realistic PL fringe / development players.
- Major European clubs prefer higher-quality players and, where appropriate, high-upside younger players.
- External clubs consider player value, wage level, age, overall, potential, reputation/standard fit and whether the player is transfer listed.
- External clubs must stay within their own abstract transfer budget and wage ceiling.
- Their budgets refresh each season because their wider finances are not fully simulated.

OFFER UI
- Incoming offer screen now shows the buying club's league/division and country.
- Inbox messages identify the buyer market, e.g. "Benfica (Primeira Liga • Portugal)".
- All existing SCR sale-impact, star-player and manager/DoF decision information remains in the offer screen.

PLAYER WORLD
- If one of your players is sold to an external club, that existing known player moves there and persists in the save.
- The game does NOT generate a squad around him.
- External clubs remain buyer-only in v0.16.2:
  - user cannot start an approach for external-club players;
  - Premier League AI does not use exported external-club players as a hidden foreign recruitment database.
- This avoids accidentally turning v0.16.2 into a large database expansion.

COMPATIBILITY
- Existing saves work.
- Existing transfer-listed players automatically begin using the expanded buyer market.
- v0.16 financial regulations retained.
- v0.16.1 listing improvements / bid safeguards / transfer withdrawal retained.


Patch v0.16.2a — Contract Renewal SCR Preview
- Existing-player contract negotiations now show Current SCR -> Projected SCR.
- Preview updates live as weekly wage or contract length changes.
- Wage changes affect annual regulated payroll immediately.
- For post-save signings, longer/shorter renewals also preview the effect of re-spreading remaining transfer + agent acquisition cost.
- Preview shows annual regulated cost added/removed and the current regulatory limit.
- Accepted renewal still applies the existing acquisition-cost restructuring logic.
