Football CEO v0.18.0 — Player Development, Dynamic Values & Living Transfer Market

BUILT ON v0.17.3

PLAYER DEVELOPMENT
- Proper career development replaces the old simple age<=24 annual roll.
- Development can occur visibly during the season at October, January and April checkpoints, plus the year-end assessment.
- Potential is a ceiling/opportunity, not a guaranteed destination.
- Development is driven by age, potential gap, actual/estimated minutes, performance, training environment and player-specific variation.
- Meaningful improvement remains possible through age 26-27; the curve tapers more strongly from 28 onward.
- Young players with strong minutes/performance can improve rapidly; annual movement is capped to keep careers believable.
- AI-controlled players receive estimated usage based on their positional squad rank so the wider football world also develops.
- Older players face variable age-based decline rather than a universal fixed-age drop.
- Player profiles show current-season OVR movement and a development status: Developing rapidly / Progressing / Stable / Stagnating / Declining.

INJURIES & DEVELOPMENT
- Injury history is stored in player-market state.
- 90+ day injuries count as long injuries and reduce future development/market confidence.
- Very long injuries (105+ days in the current injury-duration model) create a probabilistic risk of a direct OVR loss at year end.
- Older players are more vulnerable to failing to regain their previous level.
- Long injuries do not guarantee permanent decline.

DYNAMIC MARKET VALUES
- Embedded launch values are replaced in-career by a dynamic valuation model.
- Value uses OVR, age, potential/upside, club level, current-user performance, playing time, contract length and injury history.
- The OVR/value curve is deliberately steeper at the upper-middle and elite end than the old database values.
- Values are recalculated in career and persisted through player-world overrides.
- Value history is stored at season rollover.

MARKET VALUE VS EXPECTED COST
- Market value and expected transfer cost are separate concepts.
- Expected cost uses market value plus contract leverage, availability, selling-club financial pressure, club stature difference, player willingness to make a step up and same-league/direct-rival resistance.
- Strong player interest in a major upward move can soften seller resistance without producing extreme unrealistic discounts.
- Similar-strength Premier League clubs charge a competitive/rival premium.
- Player dossier now shows Market value, Expected cost range, availability and major seller-context drivers.

AI PLAYER AVAILABILITY
- AI clubs now classify players as Not for sale / Open to offers / Transfer listed.
- Availability is reviewed during transfer windows based on positional depth, age, wage burden, upside and squad rank.
- Transfer-listed players are genuine opportunities: expected midpoint is capped below market value and the displayed expected range remains below market.
- Listings are visible in the Player Database.
- AI buyers also prioritise appropriate listed/value opportunities.

LIVING AI TRANSFER MARKET
- AI transfer attempts are substantially more frequent than v0.17.3, while still requiring squad need and affordability.
- AI clubs identify needs through existing squad evaluation and now search a wider target pool.
- Listed players receive recruitment priority where they fit the buyer.
- Completed AI-to-AI Premier League transfers are now always surfaced in the news feed, not only marquee deals.
- Existing AI budgets, wage limits and SCR logic remain in force.

PLAYER DATABASE
- Market value column updated to dynamic value.
- Added Expected cost range.
- Added Availability.
- Added filters for Transfer listed / Open to offers / Not for sale.
- Added sorting by OVR, expected cost, market value, best-value discount and age.
- Players materially available below market receive a subtle Good value indicator.

- Existing v0.17.2 fitness/workload/rotation/substitution systems retained.
