Football CEO v0.19.0 — Embedded FC26 Expanded World

BUILT ON v0.18.1 + multi-league foundation

EMBEDDED PLAYER DATABASE
- 1,665 FC26 player records are stored directly inside world-players.js.
- No player-data download, API request or first-launch import occurs at runtime.
- Total launch database in the tested build: 2,212 players (547 existing PL + 1,665 expanded-world players).
- Imported world players cover all registered 2025/26 Championship, La Liga and Bundesliga clubs.
- Championship: 650 players.
- La Liga: 500 players.
- Bundesliga: 515 players.
- All 62 registered world clubs have source players; smallest source squad is 19 players.
- Source-backed fields include identity, club, nationality, age, positions, preferred foot, weak foot, skill moves, height and FC26 OVR.
- Potential, value, wage and contract expiry are Football CEO estimates because the supplied FC26 CSV does not contain those fields.

LEAGUE WORLD
- Premier League remains the only playable/full simulation league.
- Championship: 24 clubs, standard background simulation, 46 rounds.
- La Liga: 20 registered lite-simulation clubs.
- Bundesliga: 18 registered lite-simulation clubs.

CHAMPIONSHIP
- Background simulation advances through the daily Continue calendar.
- 24 clubs play 46 league matches each / 552 fixtures total.
- Competition reaches round 46 by early May.
- State resets for the next season.

TRANSFERS
- Expanded-world players appear in Player Database, manager recruitment and AI recruitment.
- Championship/La Liga/Bundesliga clubs have lightweight persistent transfer finances.
- World clubs can bid for Premier League players.
- Premier League clubs can sign world players.
- Selling world clubs receive transfer income.
- Player willingness, reputation, expected transfer cost and wages remain active.
- Existing external buyer-only clubs outside the registered leagues remain supported.

PERFORMANCE
- Player-market state initialization is cached by database size.
- Position-indexed transfer searches reduce repeated 2,000+ player scans.
- Incoming offers reuse weekly AI recruitment plans.
- AI clubs no longer run a full recruitment reassessment immediately after each completed deal.

PWA
- world-players.js is included directly in the app shell.
- Cache version: football-ceo-v0.19.0-pwa-3.
