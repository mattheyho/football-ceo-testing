Football CEO v0.24.3 — Recruitment Intelligence, XI Selection & CEO Recruitment Briefs

RECRUITMENT INTELLIGENCE
- Manager recruitment now distinguishes the role actually lost: starter, first-team competition, backup or prospect.
- Replacement quality is benchmarked against the departed player's role and level instead of automatically matching the strongest remaining player in that position.
- Existing internal replacements are recognised; a sale does not automatically trigger a signing when the squad can absorb the departure.
- Recently sold players are excluded from being recommended as their own replacement.
- Role-specific spending discipline stops depth signings from consuming starter-level transfer budgets without a strong reason.
- Backup/competition goalkeeper searches now favour either affordable younger options with enough current ability or credible experienced senior backups.
- Shortlist scoring now considers current ability, potential, positional fit, affordability, age, manager profile, interest and the importance of the vacancy.

MANAGER SHORTLISTS
- Recruitment requests now aim to present three credible strategic routes when the market supports them.
- The old fixed Direct / Value / Long-term labels have been replaced.
- The first recommendation is shown as Manager's First Choice.
- Alternatives receive contextual descriptors such as Best Fit, Value, Youth, Experienced or High Upside.
- A youth option is only presented when its ability, potential and price make sense for the role.

CEO RECRUITMENT BRIEFS
- The CEO can reject a manager shortlist and give one of three instructions:
  • Aim higher / spend more
  • Spend less
  • Different options
- Aim higher raises the quality/investment brief without allowing spending beyond the actual available playing budget.
- Spend less prioritises value and preserves more of the transfer budget while maintaining a credible role floor.
- Different options asks the manager to reassess the market without changing the financial brief.
- Rejected targets are remembered so refreshed shortlists favour genuinely different names when the market permits.
- If the CEO asks to aim higher but the current budget cannot access a clearly stronger player, the manager explains that the market ceiling has been reached rather than inventing a worse recommendation.

SQUAD SELECTION
- Manager XI selection now optimises the whole formation rather than filling positions greedily one by one.
- This preserves scarce natural-position players for the roles where they are most valuable and prevents avoidable selections such as a central midfielder at striker while a natural striker is used elsewhere.
- Emergency positional use remains possible only when a squad genuinely cannot fill the formation credibly.
