Football CEO v0.24.7 — League-Linked Youth Ecosystem

YOUTH GENERATION
- World prospects are now generated through simulated clubs rather than from one quality-agnostic global pool.
- Starting OVR is driven primarily by club academy quality and the football level of the league the player emerges from.
- Potential is influenced by academy quality and national talent depth, with only a light league effect so elite players can still emerge below the top flight.
- Exceptional 90+ potential prospects can enter senior football unusually advanced, allowing a very small number to become genuine 90+ stars by prime age.
- Generated players retain youthOriginClub, youthLeagueQuality and youthAcademyQuality for future history, debugging and pathway features.

LEAGUE QUALITY
- Each simulated league now has a youth-environment rating calculated from senior squad quality and club standards.
- League youth quality moves only 20% toward the newly observed level each season, preventing one promotion/relegation or anomalous season from instantly changing intake quality.
- The system is data-driven: when League One, League Two or further leagues are added to WORLD_LEAGUES/WORLD_CLUBS, their standards feed the same youth model automatically.

ACADEMIES / NATIONAL TALENT
- The user's actual Academy facility rating remains the direct academy-quality input for the user's intake.
- AI academy quality is estimated from club reputation with stable club-specific variance, so academy strength is not a perfect copy of league position.
- National talent depth modestly influences potential distribution.
- Lower-league clubs still retain a genuine, rare chance of producing elite or generational potential.

WORLD PIPELINE
- Annual world intake no longer waits primarily for that summer's retirements.
- The forward pipeline scales with the number of simulated clubs and the size of the active player database.
- Current loaded world target is 151 world prospects per season, plus the user's 2–4 academy players.
- In the expansion regression, adding synthetic League One + League Two (48 clubs) automatically raised the target from 151 to 168 even before their full player databases were added.
- Retirement volume can still lift intake when required, but is no longer the main trigger for creating future talent.
