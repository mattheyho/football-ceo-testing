

Patch v0.16.2c — Rejected Sale Stakeholder Fix
- Stakeholder reactions to outgoing star transfers now fire only after the player has actually transferred.
- Rejecting an incoming offer or having a buying club withdraw no longer produces "player sold" fan/owner/manager reactions.

- Pre-sale star status and SCR impact are captured before the move, then applied only after transfer completion.
- One-time migration repairs affected saves by reversing/removing false sale reactions linked to rejected incoming offers where the player is still at the club.
