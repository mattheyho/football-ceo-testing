Football CEO v0.19.3 — Recruitment & SCR Tweaks + Database Fix (cache-corrected)

RECRUITMENT
- Explicit replacement requests remember the quality of the player sold.
- Manager recruitment requests reserve the expected cost of their preferred target against available transfer budget.
- Explicit sale vacancies remain high priority and benefit from new transfer income.

SCR / PLAYER SALES
- SCR recognises estimated net profit/loss on player disposals in current-season football revenue.
- Sales also remove annual wage/amortisation cost.
- Transfer-sale previews show estimated disposal profit and annual regulated-cost savings.
- Disposal profit resets at the new-season boundary.

EXPANDED-WORLD DATABASE
- Corrected the age=25 importer fallback for all 3,067 imported Championship/European/Saudi players.
- Age is calculated from the FC26 source birthdate at 1 August 2025.
- Birthdate is retained in imported player records.
- Football CEO potential and initial market value are regenerated/rebalanced using corrected age.
- Imported ages persist through season rollover/save overrides.

CACHE / DEPLOYMENT CORRECTION
- Root cause of the live 'everyone is still 25' report was stale PWA runtime data: world-players.js kept the same URL, allowing the previous service worker to serve the old age-25 file.
- Runtime JS URLs are now build-versioned with ?v=0193-dbfix3.
- Service worker registration is versioned and cache bumped to football-ceo-v0.19.3-pwa-3.
- Runtime JS/CSS/JSON is network-first online with offline cache fallback.
- This means database updates cannot silently boot against an older cached world-players.js.

POST-MATCH LINEUP OVR
- Starting XI formation cards now show each player's OVR alongside the existing match rating/events.
- Bench rows now always show OVR; substitutes who played also retain their match rating.
- Stored reports fall back to the current player OVR if an older report does not contain an embedded OVR.
- PWA cache: football-ceo-v0.19.3-pwa-3.
