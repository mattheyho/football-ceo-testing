Football CEO v0.19.2 — Tweaks

Changes from v0.19.1:
- Premier League final match no longer ends the global football season. Calendar continues to 1 June; Season Review opens on 1 June after background leagues have completed.
- Manager recruitment requests now become reminder messages after rejection, with refreshed shortlists.
- Recruitment requests can be closed until the next transfer window; a material squad change (sale, meaningful injury/depth crisis) overrides the suppression.
- Recruitment target scoring now includes club strategic fit. Top-six/European-chasing clubs favour prime/upside players and heavily downgrade ageing short-term targets.
- Player development is more dependent on competitive minutes and performance.
- Low/no-minute youngsters no longer receive near-automatic +1 gains at every development checkpoint.
- Veteran decline is substantially softer, performance/minutes can protect against decline, and goalkeepers receive a later ageing curve.
- Background development also uses estimated playing-time role.
- PWA cache bumped to v0.19.2.
