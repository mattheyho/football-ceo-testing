Football CEO v0.19.1
Expanded Football World + Scalability Update

Baseline: v0.18.1 stable Premier League build.

Major changes:
- 3,614-player runtime: 547 existing PL players + 3,067 embedded world players.
- Background leagues: Championship, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1.
- Saudi Pro League registered as a transfer-market feature only; no Saudi table/fixtures.
- Staggered AI recruitment reviews across the week.
- Staggered background player market updates across days 1-7 of each month.
- Staggered player development windows across 1-7 Oct, Jan and Apr.
- User club development always runs on day 1 and creates a manager Development Review inbox report when ratings change.
- Development Review modal shows previous OVR, new OVR, potential and rating change.
- Continue button retains Processing spinner / double-click protection.
- New season uses a real staged progress overlay, including player updates, market values, squad cache rebuilds, background strengths, competitions and transfer-market preparation.
- Club squad / world-strength caches are invalidated only for affected clubs after transfers.
- Background players do not receive unnecessary daily PL-style fitness simulation.
- Player database pagination/performance fixes retained.
- World player data is embedded locally; no runtime fetch.

Saudi premium approaches:
- targets user-club players aged 28-35, OVR 72+
- strongest weighting around ages 31-32
- bid = expected transfer cost +12% to +28%
- major expected wage increase
- capped to an occasional special approach rather than ordinary Saudi buyer behaviour

This build does NOT yet make the background leagues playable and does NOT yet activate promotion/relegation between the Premier League and Championship.
