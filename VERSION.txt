Football CEO v0.24.1 — User Academy Intakes

USER-CLUB ACADEMY INTAKE
- The user-controlled club now receives 2–4 generated academy prospects every summer.
- Prospects are aged 16–19 and immediately become normal senior-database/squad players.
- Academy quality influences cohort strength and readiness without guaranteeing elite talent.
- Most prospects are ordinary/good; very good players are uncommon and 88+ potential prospects remain rare.
- User academy graduates receive cheap youth contracts and can be developed, sold or allowed to leave like any other player.
- Each intake is persistent in the save and rehydrates correctly after reload.
- Summer Inbox notification lists each new prospect with age, position, OVR and potential.

GENERATED NAMES
- Generated prospects use nationality-specific first-name and surname pools.
- User-club intakes are primarily drawn from the club's home nationality, with international academy recruits mixed in.
- Active-database duplicate names are avoided; long careers fall back to believable double surnames when necessary.

BALANCE
- Intake size is 2–4 players, lightly influenced by Academy rating.
- Academy rating shifts potential probabilities rather than determining fixed outcomes, so weaker academies can still very occasionally produce a top prospect.
- Existing passive youth development remains active: 16–20-year-olds with potential headroom can still gain around +1 without first-team minutes, while match time drives larger gains.

PWA / COMPATIBILITY
- Compatible with v0.24 saves; no destructive save migration is required.
- Service-worker cache advanced to football-ceo-v0.24.1-pwa-1.
