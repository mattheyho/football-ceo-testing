Football CEO v0.15.6 — Commercial & Matchday Fixes

Built on v0.15.5 CEO Briefing.

SPONSORS
- A new sponsor now starts a completely new sponsor relationship.
- Previous sponsor happiness and sponsor history do not carry over to the new company.
- New neutral/positive sponsor starts at 72 happiness.
- New fan-opposed sponsor starts at 61 happiness.
- Multi-year deals still retain their relationship during the same contract.

STAKEHOLDER DRIFT
- Persistent drivers no longer subtract/add the same happiness forever.
- Weekly drivers now create a relationship equilibrium.
- Happiness moves toward that equilibrium and stabilises if circumstances stay unchanged.
- Immediate CEO decisions still cause immediate happiness changes.
- Example: "Sponsor unpopular with supporters" (-3) naturally settles around 61 rather than causing -1 every week indefinitely.

COMMERCIAL SETUP
- Added live predicted home attendance.
- Added live predicted occupancy.
- Added live predicted match revenue.
- Projections update immediately when ticket/concession/hospitality/food prices change.
- Projection includes current supporter happiness and shows when fan unhappiness is reducing demand.

STAR PLAYERS
- Club's top five players by OVR (value tie-break) are flagged as STAR players.
- Squad screen shows a ★ STAR badge.
- Player profile shows ★ STAR PLAYER plus a warning that a sale may cause supporter unrest.
- Transfer-sale fan reaction uses the exact same star definition as the UI.

MATCH LINEUP LEFT/RIGHT DISPLAY
- Fixed the post-match pitch viewing orientation.
- RB/RM/RW now display on the right side of the pitch.
- LB/LM/LW now display on the left side.
- Added the tactical slot (RB, LB, RW, etc.) to each pitch player tile.
- The underlying v0.15.3a selection logic remains strict for natural full-backs.


Patch v0.15.6a — Star Player Transfer Warning
- Incoming transfer-offer / negotiation screen now displays ★ STAR PLAYER when applicable.
- Warning explicitly states that selling the player may cause supporter unrest.
- Sale decision stats now show Apps, Starts, Goals and AVG rating.
- Uses the same isClubStarPlayer() definition as the Squad screen, Player Profile and actual supporter-reaction logic.
