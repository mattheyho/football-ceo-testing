Football CEO v0.23.0 — Live CEO Playing Budget Allocation

LIVE PLAYING BUDGET CONTROL
- The CEO can now change the club's playing investment allocation at any point during the season from the Finance screen.
- The Finance screen shows:
  • total playing investment allocated
  • completed incoming-transfer spend already committed
  • remaining transfer budget available to recruitment
  • today's sustainable allocation ceiling
  • club cash and financial-health context
- The slider controls the TOTAL season playing allocation, while the familiar transfer budget remains the unspent balance.
- Completed transfer spend is irreversible, so the allocation can never be reduced below money already committed to completed incoming transfers.
- The allocation can be increased when club resources improve during the season, including after major player sales.
- Reducing the allocation does not create cash: it releases planned recruitment capacity so existing club cash can instead be retained, used for infrastructure, debt reduction or other club priorities.

PLAYER SALES & CEO CONTROL
- Player-sale proceeds no longer automatically increase the transfer budget.
- Upfront sale proceeds enter club cash immediately.
- Deferred sale proceeds remain transfer receivables and enter cash when instalments fall due.
- The CEO decides how much, if any, of improved club resources should be reallocated to incoming transfers.
- Example: selling a player for £120m can strengthen club cash while the transfer budget remains unchanged, allowing the CEO to prioritise a stadium project instead of automatically reinvesting the sale.

MID-SEASON MANAGER REACTION
- Meaningful playing-budget changes (roughly £10m+ and/or 15%+ of the current allocation) create a manager response.
- Significant increases improve manager sentiment and generate an Inbox update.
- Significant reductions can disappoint the manager, although reductions made during genuine financial pressure are treated more sympathetically.
- Small routine adjustments do not spam stakeholder reactions.

OWNER FUNDING SAFEGUARD
- Owner football equity remains an available resource where appropriate, but the season's owner-funding allowance can no longer be repeatedly triggered by moving the slider up and down.
- Owner football funding used during the season is tracked separately and capped against the available seasonal owner contribution.
- Financial-distress restrictions introduced in v0.22 still apply.

TRANSFER TERMS / CLUB FINANCE
- v0.22 negotiated payment terms remain unchanged:
  • 25%, 50%, 75% or 100% upfront
  • 1–3 annual instalments
  • future transfer payables and receivables
  • cash timing affects AI valuation
  • acquisition/agent costs
- Purchase headline fees still consume the playing allocation when a transfer completes.
- Only the upfront fee + acquisition costs leave club cash immediately; future instalments remain future commitments.

PREMIER LEAGUE / SCR
- The unified v0.22 finance model remains in place.
- Premier League completion revenue continues to reconcile through the Inbox settlement.
- European competition revenue remains dormant/hidden until European competitions exist in-game.
- Premier League SCR remains modelled at 85%.

SAVE COMPATIBILITY
- v0.22 saves that do not contain the new playingBudget state are migrated automatically.
- The current total allocation is reconstructed from completed transfer spend + remaining transfer budget.
- Existing transfer instalments, club cash, debt, stadium projects and season-ticket state remain compatible.
