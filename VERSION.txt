Football CEO v0.16 — Financial Regulations

Built on v0.15.6a.

CORE RULE
- Football CEO now uses a universal Squad Cost Ratio (SCR) financial-regulation system.
- Premier League limit: 70%.
- Future Championship scaffold: 80%.
- Future League One / League Two scaffold: 90%.
- Status bands:
  Healthy <= 60%
  Tight >60% to regulatory limit
  Breach above regulatory limit to 80%
  Severe above 80%

HYBRID ACCOUNTING
- Starting ratio is created by live annual football payroll plus a calibrated inherited legacy cost.
- Legacy cost represents pre-save amortisation / acquisition commitments without needing historic accounting for every player.
- The legacy burden is distributed across the existing squad so selling a player removes their share of inherited cost.
- Remaining inherited costs decay by 24% each season as old commitments run off.
- New transfers from the save start are tracked individually:
  transfer fee / contract term
  + estimated agent cost (5% of fee) / contract term
  + annual wages.
- Contract extensions for post-save signings re-spread remaining regulated acquisition cost over the new contract.
- Selling a post-save signing removes their remaining annual acquisition cost and wage from the projected ratio.

REVENUE
- Every current club has a calibrated football-revenue baseline.
- Main sponsorship moves revenue relative to the club's baseline sponsor value.
- Matchday pricing / supporter demand can move projected annual matchday revenue.
- Hooks exist for future cup / European competition revenue.

CEO TRANSFER BUDGET
- CEO now sets the transfer budget in the pre-season commercial/financial setup.
- The game shows Available Investment and allows the CEO to allocate part of it.
- The old configured transfer budget remains the default first-season allocation.
- Maximum available first-season investment is 125% of the old configured budget.
- Manager / Owner happiness reacts to aggressive or conservative allocations.
- When SCR is already over the limit, the manager is more understanding of a conservative budget.

RECRUITMENT AI
- Manager / DoF recommendations now plan around the remaining transfer budget.
- Recruitment spreads budget across multiple identified squad needs rather than assuming the full budget is for one signing.
- Manager recruitment aggression affects how far above an equal share they will push for a priority signing.
- DoF rating increases the importance of SCR impact, value and young-player upside.
- When already in breach, strong DoFs favour lower annual-cost / higher-upside investments.
- Managers no longer suggest permanent targets that exceed the total remaining transfer budget.

TRANSFER SCREENS
- Incoming signing dossier shows Current SCR -> Projected SCR.
- Agreed-fee / contract stage also shows projected regulated cost.
- Incoming offers for your players show Current SCR -> Projected SCR after sale.
- The screen shows the annual regulated cost added or removed.

CONTEXT-AWARE STAKEHOLDERS
- Star sales that materially improve an existing SCR breach receive a reduced fan penalty.
- Manager penalty is also reduced when a key player must be sold to restore compliance.
- Owners reward financially necessary corrective sales.
- Rejecting a manager transfer target while above the SCR limit creates a much smaller manager penalty.

ANNUAL ASSESSMENT / SANCTIONS
- Compliance is assessed at the season rollover rather than punishing temporary in-season fluctuations.
- <= limit: compliant, breach streak resets.
- 70-75%: warning; repeat offences can reduce next-season investment and create a fine.
- 75-85%: breach; fines and next-season investment restrictions increase with repeat offences.
- >85%: severe breach; larger fines and stronger investment restrictions.
- Repeat serious breaches can trigger a next-season transfer registration ban.
- Transfer bans block incoming permanent transfers but do NOT block selling players.
- Repeat offences escalate via consecutive-breach history.

VISIBILITY
- SCR is now a headline Dashboard metric.
- Finance screen shows:
  football revenue
  regulated squad cost
  headroom / reduction required
  annual football payroll
  inherited pre-save commitments
  post-save transfer & agent costs
  current sanctions.
- Tutorial now explains financial regulations and CEO transfer-budget allocation.

NOTE
These are Football CEO gameplay regulations inspired by real football financial controls.
They are not presented as exact UEFA SCR or Premier League PSR accounting.
