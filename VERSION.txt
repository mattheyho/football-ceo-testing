Football CEO v0.15.3 — Matchday Selection & Lineups

- Manager now selects and stores an actual XI for every user fixture.
- Selection uses the manager's preferred formation from v0.15.2.
- Player selection scores overall quality plus positional suitability.
- Full-back sides are strict:
  RB/RWB strongly fit right-back; LB/LWB strongly fit left-back.
  Opposite-side full-backs are emergency-only.
- Wide positions remain deliberately flexible:
  RM/RW can cover LM/LW and vice versa with a modest suitability penalty.
- Match stats are now generated from the manager-selected XI rather than the top 11 OVR players.
- Added Starts and Assists to player season statistics.
- Match report stores formation, XI, player ratings, goals, assists, bench and goal events.
- Post-match formation graphic appears after every user league match.
- Goal icon: football.
- Assist icon: dartboard/target.
- Historical lineups can be reopened from Fixtures using View lineup.
- Player profiles and Squad season stats now show Starts and Assists.
- Season archive now stores starts, assists and average rating.
- Bench is stored now for future substitution/manager-usage systems.

Patch v0.15.3a — Full-back Priority
- Full-back selection now distinguishes primary and secondary positions.
- Primary RB/RWB and LB/LWB receive strong priority.
- A CB/midfielder with RB/LB as a secondary position no longer displaces a fit natural full-back simply due to higher OVR.
- Wide-player cross-flank flexibility remains intentionally loose.
- Villa regression test: Cash selected at RB and Digne at LB ahead of Konsa/McGinn when all are available.
