Football CEO v0.19.3 — Recruitment & SCR Tweaks + Database Fix

RECRUITMENT
- Explicit replacement requests remember the quality of the player sold.
- A first-choice sale is no longer converted into a generic depth/competition search around the remaining backup.
- Manager requests reserve the expected cost of their preferred target against the available transfer budget.
- Managers therefore cannot stack several starter requests that all assume access to the same money.
- New transfer income immediately increases the uncommitted recruitment budget.

SCR / PLAYER SALES
- SCR now recognises estimated net profit/loss on player disposals in current-season football revenue.
- Sales still remove the player's annual wage/amortisation cost as before.
- Starting-save players use an estimated book value from Football CEO's inherited regulated-cost model.
- Players signed during the save use tracked remaining amortisation as book value.
- Transfer-sale previews show estimated disposal profit as well as annual regulated-cost savings.
- Disposal profit resets at the new-season boundary.

EXPANDED-WORLD DATABASE FIX
- Corrected the age=25 importer fallback affecting all 3,067 embedded Championship/European/Saudi players.
- The FC26 source contains birthdates for all 3,067 imported players. Age is now calculated from birthdate at the data-pack reference date of 1 August 2025.
- Birthdate is now retained in each imported player record.
- Football CEO potential is regenerated from real age + current OVR using the game's own deterministic age-based model.
- Initial embedded market values are rebalanced for corrected age and potential.
- Existing Football CEO wage estimates are retained; the source dataset does not provide wage data.
- Imported ages now persist through season rollover/save overrides rather than reverting to their launch-data age after reload.
- No runtime web/data fetching has been added.
