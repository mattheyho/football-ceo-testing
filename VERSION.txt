Football CEO v0.14.6
- Manager squad needs split into starting XI / first-team competition / backup roles
- Shortlists target the quality band appropriate to the requested role
- Backup requests deliberately avoid unnecessary superstar targets
- Up to two manager requests can be created per week
- Underperformance / multiple major needs increases chance of two requests
- Successful manager-requested signings suppress the same positional role for 12 weeks
- Sale-created vacancies remember whether a starter, competition player or backup was lost
- Manager-requested signings now explicitly fulfil that positional role for 12 weeks
- Fresh sales/injuries can reopen a previously fulfilled squad role
