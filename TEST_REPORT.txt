Important browser limitation:
localStorage is tied to the exact website origin and browser profile. A career saved at
mattheyho.github.io will not automatically appear at a Netlify preview, local file, private
browsing session, or another browser/device. Exported JSON backups are the protection against
Safari storage clearing and against moving between origins.
