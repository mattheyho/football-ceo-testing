FOOTBALL CEO — PIECE 9B.2: COMMERCIAL REACH + MOVEMENT-SAFE COSTS
Version: v0.24.28

Purpose
-------
Prepare finance architecture for future promotion/relegation without making promotion/relegation executable yet.

Implemented
-----------
1. Persistent Commercial Reach
- Commercial reach is seeded from reputation, supporter demand, attendance, stadium scale and current league exposure.
- The score changes gradually across seasons instead of resetting with division.
- Sponsor benchmark uses club reputation, fan exposure, stadium scale, financial scale, league exposure, commercial growth and sponsor relationship.
- Bigger clubs within the same division can therefore command materially different deals.
- Finance UI now shows a qualitative Commercial Reach label and current main-sponsor benchmark.

2. Sponsor examples at neutral 2025/26 state
- Bristol City: ~£3.3m benchmark
- Sheffield Wednesday: ~£3.5m
- Watford: ~£4.25m
- Leicester City: ~£6.4m
- If promoted immediately, the same benchmark rises to roughly £6.75m for Bristol and £11.75m for Leicester before longer-term brand growth.
- Individual offers still vary by term, supporter controversy and sporting performance.

3. Staff movement clauses
- New staff hires remain priced at divisional market rates.
- Existing manager/DoF/Head Physio contracts are not rewritten to full market rates on movement.
- Promotion applies a +20% contractual wage adjustment.
- Relegation applies a -15% contractual wage adjustment.
- The canonical recordLeagueMovement hook triggers the staff adjustment automatically for future movement code.

4. Facility running costs
- Training, medical and recruitment costs now depend primarily on facility quality and the club's scale/reputation.
- Division is only a modest standards modifier (PL 1.08, Championship 1.00, League One 0.92, League Two 0.86).
- Academy running costs remain primarily programme/category based, with a modest club-scale factor.
- The same Bristol example facilities rise only ~7.5% when switched from Championship to PL, rather than ~4.5x.

5. Stadium operating costs
- Fixed stadium cost is capacity-driven.
- Matchday staffing cost is driven by expected attendance plus a smaller capacity/readiness overhead.
- A large underused ground therefore remains more expensive than a small ground, while a full large ground requires more matchday staffing.
- Promotion does not reprice the physical stadium.

Scope deliberately unchanged
-----------------------------
- Promotion/relegation execution remains disabled.
- Championship play-offs remain disabled.
- League One and League Two remain non-playable.
- League Two remains the static bottom division with no National League relegation.
