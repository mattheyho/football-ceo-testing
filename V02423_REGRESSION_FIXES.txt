Football CEO v0.24.23 — Regression fixes

1. Loan inbox ownership
- Final loan reports are now generated only for players owned by the user club and loaned out.
- AI-to-AI loan completions no longer pollute the CEO inbox.
- Incoming user loans receive a simple return-to-parent notification, not a development report.
- Existing invalid AI loan reports/news are cleaned from compatible saves once on load.

2. Retired-player transfer guard
- Retired players are filtered from cached positional recruitment pools at read time.
- Transfer attainability now hard-rejects retired players and the synthetic Retired club.
- Existing invalid transfers from Retired are repaired once on load: the player is restored to Retired, the invalid ledger/news item is removed, and the AI buyer budget is refunded.

3. Manager shortlist ownership
- User-owned players who are out on loan are excluded from manager recruitment target pools.
- This prevents players such as R. Esse being recommended as external transfer targets while still owned by the user club.

4. Future transfer payment drill-down
- Future transfer payments and future transfer income are now tappable on the finance screen.
- Detail shows player, counterparty, headline fee, remaining balance, next due date, final due date, instalments remaining, and the individual payment schedule.

5. Includes the prior October/January/April development-review restoration.
