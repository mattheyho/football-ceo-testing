v0.24.1 extends the long-term player lifecycle with automatic user-club academy intakes. Every summer the CEO's club receives 2–4 persistent generated prospects aged 16–19, with quality influenced by the existing Academy rating and announced through the Inbox. v0.24 retirement, world replenishment, passive youth development and owner-performance expectations remain in place alongside the v0.23 finance, stadium, season-ticket and negotiated transfer-payment systems.
