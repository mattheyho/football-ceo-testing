v0.24.3 is a recruitment-intelligence release on top of the v0.24.2 stakeholder fixes and v0.24.1 academy-intake build. Managers now select the XI as a whole rather than greedily filling positions, replacement searches are benchmarked against the role actually lost, and shortlists present three credible routes instead of rigid direct/value/long-term labels. The CEO can reject a shortlist with an investment brief (Aim higher / spend more, Spend less, or Different options), after which the manager reassesses the market. Recently sold players are excluded from their own replacement search.


## v0.24.3
- Whole-XI manager selection optimisation prevents avoidable positional misuse.
- Role-aware replacement recruitment and spending discipline.
- Dynamic three-route shortlists: Manager's First Choice + contextual alternatives (Value, Youth, Experienced, Best Fit).
